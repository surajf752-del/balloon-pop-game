How to Play

  • Allow camera access when asked. Nothing is recorded or uploaded – tracking runs in your browser.
  • Raise a hand so the camera sees it. Your index fingertip becomes the glowing pointer.
  • Touch a balloon with the pointer – or swipe through several – to pop them.
  • ★ Golden +50 · 🌈 Rainbow +100 · ? Mystery = random reward · ❄ Frozen slows time · ! Bomb costs points.
  • Chain pops quickly for a combo multiplier.
